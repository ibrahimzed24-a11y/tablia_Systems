# SOP 01: Opening and closing checklist

## Purpose
Each branch should open at 10:00 AM with the app synced, the menu correct and today's bookings known. It should close at 11:00 PM
with every booking and WhatsApp request in a final state and nothing waiting to sync.

## Who does it
- **Opening:** the shift manager (or the first front_desk on shift), plus the kitchen lead for the kitchen part.
- **Closing:** the shift manager, plus the kitchen lead.

## Steps: opening (from 09:30)
1. Unlock the branch tablet and open **Mela Rossa Staff**. Check that the header shows your name, your role and the right branch.
2. Look at the sync badge at the top. It must be green ("Synced"). If it shows "Offline" or a number of pending changes, follow SOP 07 first.
3. **Bookings → Today**: read the list aloud to the floor team. Count the covers (guests) and give the number to the kitchen lead.
4. **WhatsApp inbox**: open every request in "New" from overnight. Handle each one with SOP 04 before 10:30 AM.
5. **Kitchen lead, Menu**: go through the items that ran out yesterday. If stock is back, switch them to Available. If not, keep them Sold out (SOP 02).
6. Corniche only: check that the Breakfast section is Available. New Damietta does not serve breakfast.
7. Check that the guest WhatsApp phone (01280060772) is charged and signed in on the front desk.

## Steps: closing (from 22:30)
1. **Bookings → Today**: mark every guest who came as **Completed** and every guest who did not come as **No-show** (SOP 03). Nothing from today may stay Pending or Confirmed.
2. **WhatsApp inbox**: every request should be Replied, Converted or Closed. Anything still New gets a reply now, or is noted in the shift log for the opening team.
3. **Kitchen lead**: items marked "Sold out until end of day" switch back by themselves at midnight. Items marked "Sold out (no end)" stay sold out; leave a note if tomorrow's opening team should check them.
4. Check that the sync badge is green, with 0 pending.
5. Lock the tablet. Do not sign out: signing out needs a new QR code (SOP 05).

## Checks
- At opening: the badge is green, today's bookings have been read out, and there are 0 New WhatsApp requests older than 30 minutes.
- At closing: no booking for today is Pending or Confirmed, the badge is green, and the tablet is locked.

## When it goes wrong
- **The app shows another branch or role:** stop and call the manager. The device may be the wrong one, or someone else's. Do not use it for bookings.
- **The app opens on the sign-in screen:** the device was revoked or signed out. Ask a manager for a new QR code (SOP 05).
- **The badge stays red with pending changes for more than 10 minutes on Wi-Fi:** follow SOP 07, "When it goes wrong".
- **The tablet is lost:** a manager revokes it straight away (SOP 05, "Revoke a lost device").

## Operations tasks (added with the operations engine)
- **Opening:** after step 7, open **Operations** and start the opening tasks assigned to you (SOP 11).
- **Closing:** before locking the tablet, submit your closing tasks for verification. The shift manager verifies or fails them before leaving.
